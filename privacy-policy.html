Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request roadside assistance or contact Bucky Towing, we collect your name, phone number, vehicle information (make, model, color, license plate), pickup location, and destination details necessary to fulfill dispatch and transport safely.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided is used exclusively to coordinate immediate roadside assistance, contact you regarding driver arrival and dispatch updates, and process invoicing and receipts.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared with emergency personnel or law enforcement when required by local municipal safety protocols.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@buckytowing.com or call 608-690-6868.
